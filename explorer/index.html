What do the numbers mean?

Probabilities are reach-weighted averages over training, not the last iteration's strategy. Regret compares an action with the current mixture, weighted by chance and opponent reach. CFR+ clips negative accumulated regrets to zero. These totals are not per-hand profit predictions.

History uses p for check/fold and b for bet/call. J < Q < K. Check-check showdowns win one net chip; called bets win two; folding loses one.
